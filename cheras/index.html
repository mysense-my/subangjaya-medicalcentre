MeetDr Thurston

Dr Thurston Erng

Consultant Cardiologist · Cardiology and Interventional Cardiology · Level 5, Outpatient Centre, SJMC

01.

His focus

Dr Thurston Erng is a Consultant Cardiologist at SJMC who specialises in interventional cardiology. That includes complex procedures for heavily calcified arteries and TAVI for narrowed heart valves.

CardiologyInterventional cardiologyComplex coronary interventionsTAVI
02.

Qualifications and training

He studied medicine at St Bartholomew's and the Royal London, University of London, then trained at St George's University Hospital in London and at Serdang Heart Centre.

Chest pain right now?

Call 999 or go to the nearest emergency department. Don't wait for a reply on WhatsApp.

  • When you walk, climb stairs or get stressed.
  • Getting out of breath faster than you used to.
  • A racing, pounding or skipping heartbeat.
  • Swollen ankles, or waking up at night short of breath.
  • Dizzy spells or fainting.
  • Feeling your heart flutter, race or skip, even at rest.
  • You're 50 or older
  • Diabetes or prediabetes
  • High blood pressure or high cholesterol
  • You smoke
  • A parent or sibling had heart disease young
