Most heart problems give a warning

before they become an emergency.

Warning signs

Book a consult if any of these sound familiar.

No symptoms?

It's still worth a check if any of these apply to you.

Chest pain right now?

Call 999 or go to the nearest emergency department. Don't wait for a reply on WhatsApp.

  • When you walk, climb stairs or get stressed.
  • Getting out of breath faster than you used to.
  • A racing, pounding or skipping heartbeat.
  • Swollen ankles, or waking up at night short of breath.
  • Dizzy spells or fainting.
  • Leg pain when you walk that eases when you stop.
  • You're 50 or older
  • Diabetes or prediabetes
  • High blood pressure or high cholesterol
  • You smoke
  • A parent or sibling had heart disease young
